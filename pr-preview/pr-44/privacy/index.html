    Hosting

    The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address, the date and time, the requested page, the referring page and your browser’s user agent to deliver the page and to protect its servers. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub’s privacy statement.

    Fonts, icons and the math and pseudocode renderers are served from this website itself, not from third-party servers.

    Consent message and cookie settings

    Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google (Google’s certified consent management platform, IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). It asks whether Google may use cookies and process data for advertising and statistics. Your choice is stored in a cookie on your device, so the message isn’t shown again on every page. Storing this choice is necessary to meet the consent requirements of the GDPR and § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021 (TKG 2021).

    You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawal doesn’t affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

    Google AdSense

    The website shows advertising from Google AdSense, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. AdSense loads on every page and receives your IP address and information about the page you’re viewing.

    What Google does depends on your choice in the consent message:

    • Consent to storage and personalised ads: Google may store and read cookies and similar identifiers on your device and show ads based on your interests (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021).
    • Consent to storage, but not to personalised ads: Google shows non-personalised ads, chosen by the page content and your coarse location. They still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention, which is why they also depend on your consent to storage.
    • No consent to storage: Google shows only limited ads, or no ads. Limited ads don’t use cookies or other storage for advertising. According to Google, they may use cookies or local storage (for example the __eoi cookie) to detect invalid traffic and fraud. Your IP address is still processed to deliver the ad. Delivering limited ads is based on our legitimate interest in financing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

    Google acts as an independent controller for its advertising services. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy. You can manage personalised advertising in Google’s ad settings.

    Google Analytics

    We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand which pages are read and how visitors find them.

    • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics loads only after you consent to statistics in the consent message (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). If you withdraw consent, Analytics stops and its cookies are deleted.
    • Outside these regions, Google Analytics loads without asking. The legal basis there is our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); Google doesn’t store IP addresses, data is kept for two months, and you can object at any time, for example with Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

    Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID>, which store a random identifier for up to two years. It processes the pages you visit, the time spent, the referring website, your device type, browser, operating system and approximate location. Google Analytics 4 doesn’t store IP addresses. Google uses them to derive the approximate location and for basic tasks such as spam detection and routing, and then discards them. We keep event data in Google Analytics for two months. Google Analytics is linked to our AdSense account to report earnings per page. Google signals is enabled: if you’re signed in to a Google account and have allowed ad personalisation there, Google may connect the visit to your account for reports across devices. You can turn this off in your Google account settings.

    Amazon affiliate links

    Links to Amazon on this website are affiliate links: as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. When you follow such a link, Amazon sets a cookie in your browser that records where you came from, so the commission can be attributed. Amazon processes this as its own controller; we only receive anonymous reports of sales and clicks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in financing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the privacy notices of Amazon.de and Amazon.com.

    YouTube videos

    Some pages embed YouTube videos in the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) of Google Ireland Limited. They don’t load with the page. Each one shows a notice and a Load video button instead. After you click, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and the page address and may store cookies or similar data on your device. The legal basis is your consent by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). See Google’s privacy policy.

    Comments (Disqus)

    Posts can have comments hosted by Disqus, Inc., San Francisco, USA. Nothing loads until you click Show comments. After the click, Disqus loads from disqus.com, receives your IP address and the page address, stores cookies and may show advertising. If you comment, Disqus stores your comment and profile as its own controller. The legal basis is your consent by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). See Disqus’s privacy policy.

    Other external content

    A few pages show images from other websites, for example Wikimedia Commons, GitHub or book covers from Amazon, or are exported notebooks that load scripts from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. When such a page loads, your browser requests these files from their servers, which receive your IP address. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing this content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

    Storage in your browser

    The website stores nothing in your browser beyond the consent choice described above and the cookies of the services listed here.

    E-mail

    If you write to us by e-mail, we process your address and message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). E-mails to fjp.at addresses are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) to our mailbox. We delete messages when they’re no longer needed, unless legal retention periods apply.

    Transfers to the United States

    Google, GitHub (Microsoft), Amazon and Cloudflare may process data in the USA. They are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to provide an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). Disqus processes data in the USA. Its data processing agreement for publishers includes the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

    Your rights

    You have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with the Cookie settings link, with effect for the future. To exercise your rights, write to ros@fjp.at.

    You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at.

    Changes

    We update this policy when the website changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
