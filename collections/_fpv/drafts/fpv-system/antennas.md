Short introduction: why antennas matter for the video link.

## Polarization

- Linear and circular polarization (RHCP and LHCP)

## Omnidirectional and Directional Antennas

- Omni antennas on the quad, patch antennas on the goggles, diversity

## Connectors

- SMA, RP-SMA and U.FL

## Antennas Used in this Build

- Lumenier AXII 2, see [components](/projects/fpv/components)
