Short introduction: the [VTX](/projects/fpv/glossar#vtx) on the quad and the video receiver in the goggles.

## Bands, Channels and Output Power

- 5.8 GHz bands and channels (Raceband)
- Output power levels and the legal limits in Austria and the EU
- Pit mode

## Control from the Flight Controller

- TBS SmartAudio on a UART, see the ports tab on the [flight controller software](/projects/fpv/flight-controller-software) page

## Video Receiver

- rapidFIRE module in the FatShark HDO

## VTX Used in this Build

- TBS Unify Pro HV, see [assembly](/projects/fpv/assembly)
