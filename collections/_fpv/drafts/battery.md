Short introduction: why [LiPo](/projects/fpv/glossar#lipo) batteries are used in FPV quads (energy density, high discharge rate).

- Nominal (3.7 V), fully charged (4.2 V) and minimum cell voltage
- Cell count (S) and pack voltage, see the table in the [glossar](/projects/fpv/glossar#battery)
- How the cell count relates to motor $K_v$, see [motor page](/projects/fpv/motor)

## Capacity and C-Rating

- Capacity in mAh and its effect on weight and [flight time](/projects/fpv/calculations/#flight-time)
- C-rating and maximum continuous current: $I_{max} = C \cdot \text{capacity}$
- Internal resistance and voltage sag

## Connectors

- XT60 main lead and balance lead
