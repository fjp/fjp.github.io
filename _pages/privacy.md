## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.
When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address, the date and time, the requested page, the referring page and your browser's user agent to deliver the page and to protect its servers.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Fonts, icons and the math and pseudocode renderers are served from this website itself, not from third-party servers.

## Consent message and cookie settings

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see a consent message from Google (Google's certified consent management platform, IAB Transparency and Consent Framework).
It asks whether Google may use cookies and process data for advertising and statistics.
Your choice is stored in a cookie on your device, so the message isn't shown again on every page.
Storing this choice is necessary to meet the consent requirements of the GDPR and § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021 (TKG 2021).

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.
Withdrawal doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

## Google AdSense

The website shows advertising from Google AdSense, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.
AdSense loads on every page and receives your IP address and information about the page you're viewing.

- **With your consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021), Google may store and read cookies and similar identifiers on your device and show personalised ads based on your interests.
- **Without consent**, Google shows only non-personalised or limited ads, which still use your IP address for delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and coarse location. Our legitimate interest in financing the website is the legal basis for this (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google acts as an independent controller for its advertising services.
See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
You can manage personalised advertising in [Google's ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand which pages are read and how visitors find them.

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics loads **only after you consent** to statistics in the consent message (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). If you withdraw consent, Analytics stops and its cookies are deleted.
- Outside these regions, Google Analytics loads without asking.

Google Analytics sets the cookies `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`, which store a random identifier for up to two years.
It processes the pages you visit, the time spent, the referring website, your device type, browser, operating system and approximate location.
Google Analytics 4 doesn't store IP addresses; Google uses them only to derive the approximate location and then discards them.
We keep event data in Google Analytics for **two months**.
Google Analytics is linked to our AdSense account to report earnings per page.
Google signals is enabled: if you're signed in to a Google account and have allowed ad personalisation there, Google may connect the visit to your account for reports across devices. You can turn this off in your [Google account settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Amazon affiliate links

Links to Amazon on this website are affiliate links: as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.
When you follow such a link, Amazon sets a cookie in your browser that records where you came from, so the commission can be attributed.
Amazon processes this as its own controller; we only receive anonymous reports of sales and clicks.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in financing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
See the privacy notices of [Amazon.de](https://www.amazon.de/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=201909010) and [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496).

## YouTube videos

Some pages embed YouTube videos in the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) of Google Ireland Limited.
When a page with a video loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and the page address.
In this mode YouTube doesn't store cookies for advertising until you play the video; when you play it, YouTube may store cookies and similar identifiers.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing videos (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for playback, your consent (§ 165(3) TKG 2021).
See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Comments (Disqus)

Posts can have comments hosted by Disqus, Inc., San Francisco, USA.
Nothing loads until you click **Show comments**. After the click, Disqus loads from disqus.com, receives your IP address and the page address, stores cookies and may show advertising.
If you comment, Disqus stores your comment and profile as its own controller.
The legal basis is your consent by clicking (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021).
See [Disqus's privacy policy](https://disqus.com/privacy-policy/).

## Other external content

A few pages show images from other websites, for example Wikimedia Commons, GitHub or book covers from Amazon, or are exported notebooks that load scripts from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
When such a page loads, your browser requests these files from their servers, which receive your IP address.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing this content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Storage in your browser

The website stores nothing in your browser beyond the consent choice described above and the cookies of the services listed here.

E-mails to fjp.at addresses are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) to our mailbox.

You can withdraw consent at any time with the **Cookie settings** link, with effect for the future.
To exercise your rights, write to [ros@fjp.at](mailto:ros@fjp.at).

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
